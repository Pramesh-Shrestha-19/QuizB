Brand Guidelines

This guide defines the visual language, design style, and principles that shape a clear and consistent brand experience, no matter the team or area of expertise.

At its core, QuizB is about curiosity and clarity—just like our mission to make learning engaging, fun, and rewarding. This guide lays out the essential design standards that bring our brand to life, from our color palette and typography to accessibility guidelines and visual consistency.

01

Brand Strategy

In the world of learning and entertainment, curiosity often goes unchallenged, and knowledge remains untapped.QuizB exists to transform every moment into an opportunity for discovery, engagement, and growth.

QuizB restores confidence in fun education.

We don’t just provide questions—we craft experiences that spark curiosity, test understanding, and celebrate learning. Our goal is to make quizzes more than a game; we make them a journey where users can learn, compete, and feel accomplished. By combining variety, challenge, and fun, QuizB turns casual quizzing into a habit of smart exploration.

At QuizB, we don’t just ask questions—we empower learners to grow with confidence and curiosity. Because when knowledge is unlocked, every challenge becomes an opportunity to shine.

02

Personality

QuizB’s voice brings our brand to life through every question, hint, and explanation we deliver. The way we communicate with users turns learning into an engaging, enjoyable, and confidence-building experience. Through clear, friendly, and encouraging language, we make quizzes accessible, fun, and rewarding for everyone—whether you’re testing knowledge or discovering something new.

Tone & Voice

Our Vision: why we exist
To create a world where everyone can explore knowledge, challenge themselves, and grow their curiosity.
Our Mission: what we do
Deliver engaging quizzes that educate, entertain, and inspire learning across topics.
Our Promise: how we help
Empower users to learn confidently, enjoyably, and with a sense of achievement every time they play.
03

Logo

The QuizB logo is a dynamic, playful question mark integrated with a subtle upward arrow, symbolizing curiosity, growth, and the joy of discovering knowledge. The upward motion represents our core mission—turning every question into an opportunity to learn and improve.

Designed with clean, modern lines, the logo conveys clarity and approachability, while its lively form suggests fun, engagement, and continuous exploration.

05

Typography

QuizB uses the Poppins font family, offering a modern, clean, and highly readable typeface that works seamlessly across web, mobile, and app interfaces.

Poppins’ geometric sans-serif design ensures clarity and legibility, while its friendly, approachable style reflects QuizB’s personality—making learning fun, accessible, and enjoyable for all users.

06

Art Direction

QuizB’s visual style emphasizes vibrant colors, clear layouts, and playful accents to create engaging, memorable experiences. Bold contrasts guide the user’s focus, while fun, energetic visuals make learning and quizzing feel exciting and approachable. Every design element—from buttons to icons—supports clarity, interaction, and a sense of achievement.

Clean & Professional Design

QuizB’s design combines high-contrast black backgrounds with gold accents to create a sleek, modern, and visually striking experience. Layouts are clean, well-organized, and prioritize readability, ensuring users can focus on questions, answers, and progress effortlessly. This bold, contemporary style reflects QuizB’s premium yet approachable personality—making learning feel both exciting and rewarding.
